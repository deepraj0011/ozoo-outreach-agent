# Ozoo.ai knowledge base

## What Ozoo.ai is
- Ozoo.ai is the conversation AI product line of Habilelabs, an engineering company founded in 2016 and based in Jaipur, India.
- Habilelabs has worked with more than 200 clients in more than 15 countries.
- Ozoo.ai is built only for lenders and insurers: banks, NBFCs, housing-finance companies, insurers, fintech lenders and collections agencies in India.
- In one line: talk to every borrower, and check every call.
- Habilelabs is an AWS Advanced Tier Services Partner. That is a Habilelabs partner status, not a certification of Ozoo.ai.

## Products
Ozoo.ai has three products. They share one memory, one scoring system and one set of guardrails, so a borrower's history follows them across calls and chats.

### 1. Post-Call Analytics (call quality and compliance)
- Transcribes, summarises and classifies every recorded call, and scores it against the lender's own QA scorecard.
- Transcripts in Hindi, Hinglish and English, with the agent and the customer separated.
- Every scorecard mark points to the exact moment in the transcript that supports it.
- The lender defines fatal and critical compliance rules. A stricter second check re-reviews those flags.
- Coaching: calls are grouped from fatal to above baseline, with agent leaderboards.
- Finds buying signals in calls, such as interest in a top-up loan, a balance transfer or a new loan.
- Manual QA teams typically review only 2–5% of calls. Post-Call Analytics reviews all recorded calls in scope.

### 2. Voice AI agents (AI calling)
- AI calling agents for lenders in Hindi, English and other Indian languages.
- Use cases: EMI reminders, collections, KYC follow-up, lead qualification, renewals, and replacing an inbound IVR.
- Handles interruptions, detects voicemail, and follows retry and pacing rules, with DND respected.
- Captures consent with timestamped logs. Recording disclosure and consent prompts can be set per campaign.
- Captures promises to pay and sends them to the lender's CRM.
- Hands a call to a live agent when needed.
- Works with existing telephony: SIP trunks, cloud PBX and contact-centre platforms.

### 3. Conversation AI agents (chat)
- AI agents that chat with borrowers on WhatsApp Business, web chat, in-app chat and SMS, with one conversation history across channels.
- Answers come from the lender's own documents, FAQs and policy PDFs.
- Hands a conversation to a person, or to a voice call, with the transcript attached.
- Use cases: KYC document chasing, EMI queries, statement requests and lead capture.

## Who it's for
- Good fit: banks, NBFCs, housing-finance companies, insurers, fintech lenders and collections agencies in India that run collections, tele-sales, onboarding or service calls.
- Typical buyers: Head of Collections or Recovery, Head of Sales or Tele-sales, Head of Onboarding or Operations, CX or Service Head, Head of Retention or Portfolio.
- Not a fit: companies outside lending and insurance. Ozoo.ai is built only for financial services.

## How engagement works
- Most lenders start with Post-Call Analytics on the call recordings they already have, then add one voice journey, then chat.
- Post-Call Analytics is bought on AWS Marketplace and installed into the lender's own AWS account in the Mumbai region. The setup itself takes under an hour. Most teams set up their scorecards and agents within a week.
- Voice and chat agents are scoped to the lender's programme and quoted per deployment, with a written proposal.
- A pilot starts with a 30-minute scoping call. Then we write a pilot plan for one portfolio, one DPD bucket or one journey, with success metrics agreed up front.
- After go-live, customers keep a named contact instead of a ticket queue.

## Pricing (no amounts: hand any amount question to a person)
- Post-Call Analytics has published plans that differ only in analysed call minutes per month: Starter (25,000 minutes), Growth (100,000), Business (500,000) and Enterprise (2,000,000). Every plan includes every feature.
- Prices, contract lengths and the overage rate are listed on AWS Marketplace. Minutes beyond the plan are billed per minute, and extra packs of 25,000 minutes are available.
- Terms: month to month, or 12, 24 or 36 months. INR billing is available through a private offer on AWS Marketplace.
- AWS infrastructure and the speech-to-text provider are billed to the customer separately.
- Free trial: 30 days of the Starter plan with no software charge, for buyers in India. It does not turn into a paid plan automatically.
- Voice and chat agents have no published price. They are quoted after scoping.

## Security and data
- Post-Call Analytics data stays in the customer's own AWS account in Mumbai. Habilelabs has no access to the recordings, transcripts or results.
- Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Traffic is HTTPS only.
- Transcript text masks names, phone numbers, account numbers, Aadhaar, PAN and dates of birth. The audio recording itself is not redacted.
- Role-based access, an audit trail of QA overrides and admin changes, and data retention set from 30 days to four years.
- Ozoo.ai does not yet hold a third-party security certification such as ISO 27001 or SOC 2.
- A data processing agreement and the sub-processor list are available on request. The DPA is signed before any production data is processed.
- Under the DPDP Act, Ozoo.ai supports consent duties with disclosure prompts, masking and retention settings. The lender's compliance team decides what applies to them.

## Results
- Proof of concept with an Indian NBFC handling about 1,500 recovery and service calls a day: 100% of recorded calls in scope were analysed (against a 2–5% sample before), 18 compliance flags were found that the manual sample had missed, 300 long-pending cases came up in the first pass, and 150 upsell and retention signals were found.
- These are counts from the proof of concept, not audited business results.

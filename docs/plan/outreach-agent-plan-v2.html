Sending rules

  • Business hours in the lead's time zone; no weekends or holidays.
  • Every email: booking link + one-click unsubscribe; 1–2 tracked links.
  • Never write "I saw you opened / clicked".
  • Mailbox paused if bounces > 2% or spam > 0.3%.

Stages 3–5 · Book, hand off, nurture

One lead, one state, a clear exit

Only good-fit leads reach the PM. The agent's job ends at handoff; nurture always has a way out.

Qualify + book

3 questionssize · use case · timeline→
Fit?
highPM demo
mediumSales call
lowNurture
Invite + reminders 24 h and 1 h before. No-show → reschedule message; no answer twice → nurture.

Handoff to sales

  • Agent writes a summary (emails, chat, answers, score) and alerts sales in Teams.
  • Sales accepts or rejects within 24 h; a reject needs a reason → nurture.
  • SLA missed → escalate to the manager.
  • Accepted → sales owns it; outreach stops. Won / lost reasons come back to improve messaging.

Nurture — always with exits

1 useful email every 3–4 weeks. The lead leaves when:
  • they reply → classifier · visit pricing or book → qualify
  • their "later" date arrives → re-engage message
  • they unsubscribe → suppress
  • 6 months with no activity → archive

Lead states — one at a time, across both channels

Account rule — when a paused colleague restarts

Example: the CEO engaged, so the CTO is paused.
CEO's outcomeCTO
Still talkingRestarts after 14 quiet days
Qualifying / booked / with salesStays paused — sales owns the account
"Not now"Restarts on the CEO's date (else 90 days)
Sales rejected · wrong personRestarts now
Not interested · unsubscribed · low fitRestarts after 90 days
Two active colleagues → wait for the later one. A restarted lead continues its own sequence where it stopped.
